# AI SDLC Promise and Four Areas

Source: [AI SDLC Promise and Four Areas](https://chatgpt.com/space/page_bf0e0412a6448191882216af183595bc). Retrieved through Pages on 2026-10-01; source sequence 10, last updated 2026-10-01T14:19:35.377058Z. The source Page is unchanged.

## Overall promise

Help people and AI work together to understand a software system, prepare for the intended work, deliver verified changes, and keep useful knowledge current.

People guide intent, priorities, and consequential decisions. AI investigates, performs authorized work, and provides understandable results with supporting evidence.

The process should make work easier to start, continue across sessions, review, and hand over—without requiring people to manage every implementation detail.

## The four areas

| Area | Purpose | What it provides |
| - | - | - |
| **Repository Readiness** | Establish whether the intended work can be performed and verified. | Discoverable sources, clear working instructions, usable environment and access, verification paths, and visible limitations. |
| **Knowledge Management** | Establish and maintain understanding that humans and AI can use. | Product and engineering explanations connected to behavior, code, tests, evidence, and known uncertainty. |
| **Delivery Workflow** | Move a request through to an accepted and delivered outcome. | Defined scope, requirements, planning, implementation, verification, review, and delivery responsibilities. |
| **AI Execution** | Carry out the work reliably and efficiently. | Context selection, tool use, session continuity, model choices, and appropriate investigation, implementation, and checking mechanisms. |

These areas work together throughout an effort. They are connected responsibilities, not four sequential stages or necessarily four separate skills.
